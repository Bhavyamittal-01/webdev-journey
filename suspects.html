SUSPECT PROFILES
Meet the main suspects in the Diamond Heist case...

🔍 SUSPECT #001

MR. ARNAV MEHTA
Role: The Main Suspect!

👤  Background:
A wealthy businessman who frequently visits the diamond museum
and owns a private collection of rare gemstones.

💰 Motive:
He was facing financial difficulties and wanted to acquire the priceless diamond.

🕒  Alibi:
He claims he was attending a private business meeting on the night of the theft.

🚨  Suspicious Clue:
He was seen near the museum shortly before the robbery, and his financial problems
were recently discovered.

🔍 SUSPECT #002

MS. RIYA SHARMA
Role: The Insider

👤  Background:
A museum employee who understands the security system and
has access to restricted areas.

💰 Motive:
She may have been offered money in exchange for helping someone steal the diamond.

🕒  Alibi:
She claims she had completed her shift before the diamond disappeared.

🚨  Suspicious Clue:
Her access card was discovered near the crime scene. Her shift had ended, but she still had access to the museum.

🔍 SUSPECT #003

THE MYSTERIOUS VISITOR
Role: The Stranger

👤  Background:
An unidentified person who was spotted visiting the museum shortly before closing time.

💰 Motive:
Nobody knows the person's real intention or connection to the diamond.

🕒  Alibi:
No verified alibi has been discovered.

🚨  Suspicious Clue:
A mysterious footprint was found near the exhibition room, but the visitor's identity remains unknown.
